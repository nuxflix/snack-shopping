# Menu feeds

Photos come from [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php).
Extra snacks, prep time, and ratings come from [DummyJSON recipes](https://dummyjson.com/docs/recipes).
Market items and their dollar prices come from [DummyJSON groceries](https://dummyjson.com/docs/products).
Shelf prices matched by name come from [Open Food Facts Prices](https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/).
